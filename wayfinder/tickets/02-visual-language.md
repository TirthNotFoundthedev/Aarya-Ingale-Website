# Visual language: palette, motifs, fonts

## Question

What does the site look like? Start with clean illustration vs paper cutout, and light vs dark background (the sketch colours are placeholders). That means a pink-led Bandhani palette, whether Maharashtrian motifs (e.g. Paithani peacock/lotus borders, nauvari drape) mix in and how, which pattern each scene's Ring uses, the paper texture/shadow treatment, and the artistic display font for "Aarya Ingale" plus a body font. Resolve it with a style tile to react to.

- **"Hi!" bubble:** A's style.
- **Ring:** none of the prototype mandalas were chosen. The concentric bands turning in opposite directions at different speeds are confirmed. The actual design moves to its own ticket: [Ring mandala design](06-ring-mandala.md).
